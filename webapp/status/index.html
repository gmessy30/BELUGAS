LOADING…
If you're on the water
  • Keep at least 300 feet (100 yards) away at all times.
  • Delay launching until they've left the area.
  • Steer clear of their path; shift to neutral if it's safe to do so.
  • Let them pass before continuing upriver.
  • Operate at slow, no-wake speed near belugas.

Cook Inlet belugas are endangered, and harassing them violates federal law (Marine Mammal Protection Act). Report violations to NOAA Enforcement: (800) 853-1964.
