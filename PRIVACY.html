Privacy Policy for BELUGAS

Last updated: September 2026

BELUGAS (Beluga Environmental Logging Using GPS And Smartphones) is a community wildlife-safety app for reporting and viewing beluga whale sightings in Cook Inlet, Alaska. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it's used.

What we collect

Sighting reports. When you report a beluga sighting, the app collects the photo you take, the GPS location of the sighting, and the direction the animal was traveling. This information is displayed publicly, to all users, on the in-app sightings map and list. It is not private once submitted.

Device location (for alerts). The app uses your device's location to determine which sighting alerts are relevant to you (for example, sightings near a zone or point you've subscribed to). This is used only to decide what to notify you about -- the app does not build a history or profile of where you've been, and your location is not shared with other users or displayed publicly.

Push notification token. If you enable notifications, the app registers a device token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) so it can send you sighting alerts.

Optional observer verification. By default, your device is identified only by a randomly generated ID that is not tied to your real identity. If you choose to become a verified observer, you may enter a code (provided to you directly by the app's developer) that links your device's random ID to your name and contact information. This link is stored securely and is never made public -- it is not shown to other users, not included in the public data export described below, and is used only internally to assess the trustworthiness of sighting reports.

Public data

Beluga sighting data (photos, locations, and travel direction -- never device or identity information) is available for the public to download and use, in support of Cook Inlet beluga conservation and boater safety. This export never includes anything that could identify who submitted a report.

What we don't do

We do not track your movements over time, build a profile of your activity, sell your data, or share it with advertisers. We do not require an account or login to use the app's core features.

Third-party services

The app is built on Supabase (database and backend hosting) and Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications). These providers process data on our behalf as described above and have their own privacy policies.

Data retention

Sighting reports remain available on the public map/list and in the public export unless removed by the app's developer. Device identifiers and any linked observer verification data are retained until you request their removal (see Contact, below).

Your choices

  • Location and camera access can be denied or revoked at any time in your device's settings; core sighting-viewing features will still work, but reporting a sighting or receiving proximity alerts will not.
  • You may request removal of your device's data, including any observer verification link, by contacting us below.

Children's privacy

BELUGAS is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy or requests regarding your data can be sent to: keeneyeapps@gmail.com

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised, and significant changes will be noted here.
